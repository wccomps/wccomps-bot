Authentik Role Synchronization

Synchronize Discord roles based on Authentik group membership.

  • Linked users get the Discord role for each of their Authentik groups (e.g., WCComps_GoldTeam), and team members get their team role and Blueteam
  • Anyone else holding one of those roles loses it, including members who haven't linked; they get it back once they link and are in the group or on the team
  • This also runs automatically every 5 minutes
